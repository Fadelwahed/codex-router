- **ChatGPT accounts can be given a display label.** The label is stored on the existing account record, so the account id and login stay the same. A label the operator sets is shown even when it matches the generated “ChatGPT account N” pattern; an untouched generated label still yields the email. The label field shows how many of its 120 characters are used and says when that limit is reached. Clearing a custom label restores the generated name instead of dropping the account number, and the email stays visible beside it. A long name on the account row truncates, with the full label available on hover. Enter in the rename field saves it.
