- **ChatGPT account rows show when each usage window resets.** A future reset time is rendered as a compact relative time, such as “resets in 2d 4h”, on the primary window and on a second window beside it. Day-scale labels use days and hours, rounding leftover minutes to the nearest hour (2 days 30 minutes reads “2d 1h”; under 30 minutes with no hour reads “2d 0h”). Shorter countdowns floor whole minutes, and “<1m” is shown only while time remains. A missing, non-finite, or negative reset time is left blank; once a reset instant has passed, the row says usage is refreshing instead of keeping the previous percent, and asks for a new probe. Named periods follow the Control Center language (“weekly” / “monthly” / “current”, “每周” / “每月” / “当前”, “每週” / “每月” / “目前”), including a window whose duration is missing, zero, or negative. While Settings is visible, the next countdown update is scheduled at the next minute boundary or the reset instant, whichever comes first. A usage window that is not a plain object, or whose used percent is missing or non-finite, is omitted and the row says usage is unavailable.
