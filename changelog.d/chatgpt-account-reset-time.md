- **ChatGPT account rows show when each usage window resets.** A future reset time is rendered as a compact relative time, such as “resets in 2d 4h”, on the primary window and on a second window beside it. Day-scale labels use days and hours, rounding leftover minutes to the nearest hour (2 days 30 minutes reads “2d 1h”; under 30 minutes with no hour reads “2d 0h”). A missing, non-finite, negative, or past reset time is left blank.
